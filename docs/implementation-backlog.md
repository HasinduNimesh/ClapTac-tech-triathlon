## External dependencies

- Official competition datasets and any source schema are needed to validate official-data import and forecast quality.
- An accountable Waypoint data owner/privacy reviewer must set production privacy purposes, retention periods, subject-rights handling, and shared-device policy; no legal/compliance claim should be made from the prototype inventory alone.
- Driver field research/device testing is needed to validate stopped-state interaction. Motion/geolocation gating must not be added without a privacy-reviewed product decision.
- Approved training/evaluation data, competition rules, and model artifacts are needed for calibrated predictions and NFR-19 drift monitoring. SMS provider credentials, templates, and product approval are required before T19 can be exercised end-to-end.
- A production cluster, DNS name, TLS certificate, identity provider, production credentials, durable storage, and target resource limits are needed for live production deployment, policy, privacy, performance, and recovery acceptance.
- Training datasets/model artifacts and competition rules for Datathon-derived prediction outputs are needed before claiming calibrated ETA or demand forecasts. The planner must remain functional without these inputs.
